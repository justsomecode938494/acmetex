E20 PTFE Kevlar Sewing Threads

Acmetex PTFE coated E-glass fiberglass sewing threads are made from continuous “E” glass filament yards, resulting in a strong, chemical resist textile sewing thread.

Applications

This product is ideal for situations requiring both high-temperature performance and chemical resistance. Key industries include:

  • Filter bags for chemical processing
  • Sealing & gasketing in corrosive atmospheres
  • Protective clothing / gloves exposed to chemical spatter
  • Insulation stitching in industrial furnaces
  • Expansion joints in chemically aggressive or high-temperature settings
  • High temperature hoses and liners

Technical Properties

Below are the specifications for E20 PTFE Kevlar Sewing Threads:
