Silica Sewing Threads

The Silthread12 silica sewing thread has a high tensile strength which provides excellent sewing properties. The sewing thread has a high heat resistance. Its continuous working temperature reaches 1800°F (982°C), and its instant working temperature reaches 2552°F (1400°C).

Applications

Common applications include:

  • Sewing seams for fire blankets and curtains
  • Stitching insulation covers, expansion joints
  • Protective clothing stitching that requires high temperature stability
  • Heat shields and panels where joint integrity is critical
  • Industrial tarps and covers exposed to radiant heat
