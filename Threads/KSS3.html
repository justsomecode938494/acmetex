KSS3/3 Kevlar with Stainless Steel Sewing Thread

The Acmetex Acme-KSS3/3 Para-Aramid (Kevlar by DuPont) with 316 stainless steel sewing thread combines exceptional tensile strength with advanced resistance to heat and chemicals. The Kevlar fibers provide unmatched durability, while the inclusion of high-grade 316 stainless steel ensures excellent chemical resistance and stability in extreme environments. This product is designed for industries requiring superior performance under high-temperature and heavy-duty conditions.

Applications

This sewing thread is engineered for demanding industrial uses where traditional threads would fail. Key applications include:

  • Welding blankets and protective curtains
  • Expansion joints
  • High temperature insulation systems
  • Heat shields and containment systems
  • Gaskets and over-seal doors
  • Removable insulation covers
  • Industrial filter bags

Technical Properties

The KSS3/3 sewing thread delivers consistent performance under high stress and heat. Below are the key specifications:

Property Specification
Material Para-Aramid with 316 stainless steel wire
Twist 3 ply with 3 wire
Tensile Strength 27 lbs
Final Twist “Z”
Para-Aramid Decomposition Point Approx. 878°F (470°C)
Stainless Steel Melting Point Approx. 2552°F (1400°C)
Thread Size 0.011”
Weight per Spool About 1.1 lbs (500 grams)
