24 oz Black Acrylic Fiberglass Cloth

24 oz Black Acrylic Fiberglass Cloth is made from a fiberglass base material that is coated on both sides with our custom neoprene-acrylic rubber blend. This coating enhances the fabric’s durability against abrasion, punctures, and tearing, while also offering superior resistance to water, chemicals, and UV exposure compared to standard fiberglass cloth.

Applications

This product is designed for a wide range of industrial and high-temperature applications:

  • Welding blankets and curtains
  • Spark Containments
  • High-temperature Insulation
  • Fire Blankets
  • Expansion Joints
  • Heat Shields/Containment
  • Removable Insulation Covers
