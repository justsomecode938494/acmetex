35oz w/304 Inserted Vermiculite Fiberglass Cloth

This product combines vermiculite coated fiberglass cloth with 304 stainless steel inserted fibers for enhanced mechanical durability, heat resistance, and longevity under demanding industrial conditions. The 304 inserts help improve abrasion resistance and protect the fabric in contact with metal edges or where additional reinforcement is needed.

Applications

This cloth is suited for:

  • Welding blankets and curtains with reinforced edges
  • Heat shields where mechanical wear is expected
  • Industrial insulation where cut resistance or handling strength is required
  • Expansion joints involving metal contact
  • Heavy-duty removable insulation covers
  • Safety barriers around molten metal or sparks
