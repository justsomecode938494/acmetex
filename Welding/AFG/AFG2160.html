21 oz Aluminized Fiberglass Cloth

The 21oz Aluminized Fiberglass Cloth is woven from continuous texturized E-fiberglass yarns, offering excellent chemical and fire resistance. An aluminum foil layer is laminated on one side, enhancing its reflective properties and durability for use in high-temperature and protective applications.

Applications

This product is designed for a wide range of industrial and high-temperature applications:

  • Welding blankets and curtains
  • Expansion joints
  • High-temperature insulation
  • Heat shields and containment
  • Removable insulation covers
  • Fire blankets
