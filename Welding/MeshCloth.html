304 Stainless Steel Mesh Cloth

The 304 stainless-steel results in a mesh that is extremely sturdy and flexible. Its robust properties combined with its ability to bend and assume different angles makes it ideal in many insulation applications. It possesses good resistance to high temperatures and corrosive compounds.

Applications

  • Removable covers and blankets
  • Marine Industry
  • Architectural applications
  • Industrial/commercial pad construction
