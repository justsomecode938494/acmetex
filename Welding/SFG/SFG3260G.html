32oz Grey Silicone Fiberglass Cloth

The 32oz Grey Silicone Fiberglass Cloth is a heavy-duty, high-performance fabric engineered for demanding industrial and high-temperature applications. Built with a strong fiberglass foundation and finished with a tough silicone coating, it provides superior thermal resistance, making it well-suited for areas exposed to intense heat or occasional flame contact.

Applications

This product is designed for a wide range of industrial and high-temperature applications:

  • Welding blanket and curtains
  • Expansion joints
  • High temperature insulation
  • Heat shield and containment
  • Removable insulation cover
  • Fire blankets
