17oz Red Silicone Fiberglass Cloth

The 17oz Red Silicone Fiberglass Cloth is a resilient, high-performance fabric built for demanding industrial and high-heat conditions. Constructed from a sturdy fiberglass base and finished with a durable silicone coating, it delivers strong thermal resistance, making it well-suited for applications involving elevated temperatures or occasional flame exposure.

Applications

This product is designed for a wide range of industrial and high-temperature applications:

  • Welding blankets and curtains
  • Expansion joints
  • High-temperature insulation
  • Heat shields and containment
  • Removable insulation covers
  • Fire blankets

Technical Properties

Below are the detailed specifications of this product:

Property Specification
Base Cloth Content 100% E-Fiberglass Yarn / Satin
Tensile Strength Warp: 300 lb/in (53.58 kg/cm)
Fill: 225 lb/in (40.19 kg/cm)
Temperature Resistance Base Cloth: 1000°F (540°C)
Coating: 500°F (260°C)
Fabric Finish Red Silicone Coated / Impregnated
Weight 17oz/yd² ± 10%
Thickness 0.016” ± 10%
Fire Rating Classification Class A
Flame Spread Rating 0 – 25
UV Resistance 1000 hrs – No change in tensile strength
Size 40”, 60” & 72” × 50 yards
