18 oz Fiberglass Cloth

The 18oz plain fiberglass cloth is woven from continuous texturized E-fiberglass yarns, offering excellent chemical and fire resistance.

Crafted from continuous, texturized E-glass fibers, Acmetex fiberglass cloth delivers outstanding durability along with exceptional chemical and flame resistance for demanding applications.

Applications

This product is designed for a wide range of industrial and high-temperature applications:

  • Welding blankets and curtains
  • Expansion joints
  • High-temperature gaskets
  • Fireproofing applications
  • Insulation for industrial ovens
  • Protective covers for equipment

Technical Properties

Below are the detailed specifications of this product:

Property Specification
Content/Weave 100% E-Fiberglass Yarn / Plain
Fabric Finish Plain or 9383 (Heat Cleaned)
Weight 18 oz/yd²
Thickness 0.030”
Temperature Resistance 1000°F (540°C)
