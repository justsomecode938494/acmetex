Teflon Fiberglass Cloth

Meets the requirements of ASTM C1695-09 Standard Specification for fabrication of flexible Removable and Reusable Insulation for Hot Service. Acmetex TFG1760 cloth consists of satin weave fiberglass cloth coated with PTFE. It provides excellent chemical resistance, tensile strength, and temperature exposure capabilities. Primarily used in petrochemical and aluminum industries.

Applications

  • High temperature insulation
  • Removable insulation
  • Removable covers and blankets
  • Pipe insulation
  • Aluminum industries
  • Petrochemical industries
