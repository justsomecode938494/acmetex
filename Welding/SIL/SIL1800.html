18oz Silica Cloth

The 18 oz silica cloth is woven from high quality silica yarn, providing excellent resistance to high temperatures, thermal shock, and chemicals. Its lightweight weave allows for flexibility while maintaining strength and insulating properties.

Applications

This product is suitable for a wide variety of industrial and high-temperature applications, including:

  • Furnace linings and insulation
  • Fire protection equipment
  • Removable insulation covers
  • High temperature gasketing/sealing
  • Expansion joints
  • Heat shields and barriers
  • Safety clothing or gloves (as applicable)
