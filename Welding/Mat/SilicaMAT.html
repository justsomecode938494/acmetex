Silica Insulation Mat

The Silica Insulation Mat is a specially designed needled silica insulation mat that has undergone a secondary pre-shrinking procedure. The mechanically bonded amorphous silica fibers used make it well suited for aerospace, automotive, and other critical high-temperature industrial applications.

This silica mat is environmentally friendly as it contains no harmful Refractory Ceramic Fibers (RCF). It can be easily fabricated into a wide range of textile parts thanks to its strength and handling properties. The pre-shrinking process ensures very minimal shrinkage when exposed to extreme heat.

Applications

This product is designed for a wide range of industrial and high-temperature applications:

  • Aerospace industries
  • Automotive industries
  • Industrial applications
  • Building and construction
  • Appliances and electronics
  • Power generation
