About Us

Who We Are

Acmetex Inc. was founded in 2000 with a mission to provide high-quality, high-temperature textile solutions to companies worldwide. For over two decades, we have partnered with industries across North America, Asia, and beyond, delivering innovative and cost-effective products that meet the most demanding applications.

With strategically located warehouses in North America and China, we pride ourselves on offering fast delivery, reliable service, and strong customer relationships that stand the test of time.

Our Mission & Values

  • Innovation: Continuously improving our products to meet evolving industry needs.
  • Reliability: Ensuring that our solutions perform under the toughest conditions.
  • Customer Focus: Providing responsive service, cost-effective options, and global reach.
  • Quality: Delivering products backed by certifications and trusted worldwide.

Our Products

Acmetex offers a wide range of FM-Approved welding blankets and welding curtains designed to provide maximum protection in Hot Work operations. Our extensive product line includes models such as:

  • Welding Blankets: FG2626, FG3788, ACMESIL36, FG64, FG72, VFG35
  • Welding Curtains: FG3784, Acmesil18, FG18, FG24, FG30, FG35, FG52, NFG24, NFG28, NFG30, SFG17, SFG32, VFG25, VFG18, VFG30, NFG16

Each product is engineered to meet safety standards, providing durable and dependable solutions for industries that rely on superior fire and heat protection.

Why Choose Acmetex?

With more than 20 years of industry expertise, Acmetex is your trusted partner for heat-resistant textiles. Whether you are looking for reliable welding blankets, protective curtains, or custom solutions, we are here to support your business.
