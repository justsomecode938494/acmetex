Leading Competitors in High-Temperature Textiles
1. Insulcon Group

Location: Belgium (manufacturing in Temse)

Specialties: Offers a wide range of textile products including yarns, woven and knitted fabrics, sleeving, packing, and seals made from E-Glass, ceramic fibers, silica, and more. Production includes weaving, braiding, knitting, twisting, etc.

The Insulcon Group

2. Auburn Manufacturing Inc. (AMI)

Location: USA (Maine)

Specialties: High-performance textiles for extreme heat protection—used in industries like oil, petrochemical, glass, mining, nuclear, and shipbuilding. Produces fire- and heat-resistant fabrics, blankets, and curtains.

Notable: Supplies to U.S. Navy and experiences growing demand, especially from defense and aerospace sectors.

Auburn Manufacturing, Inc.
Wikipedia

3. Goodyear Rubber Products

Location: USA

Specialties: High-temp tapes, ropes, cloth, yarns, welding blankets, curtains, insulated products—materials withstand up to ~2300 °F (≈1260 °C), with coatings like silicone, vermiculite, PTFE, and aluminized finishes.

Goodyear Rubber Products (ENG)

4. Newtex Industries

Location: USA

Specialties: High-temperature textiles (fiberglass-based; brands: Zetex, ZetexPlus, DualMirror). Offers fire containment systems, industrial seals, and safety apparel. Known for being asbestos-free and ISO 9001 certified.

Newtex Industries

5. Davlyn Group

Location: USA

Specialties: Industrial fabrics for extreme conditions—aluminized fabrics, silica fabrics, ceramic cloths, fire blankets, needled blankets, and high-performance Thermoglass® varieties.

Davlyn Group

6. Alphasource International

Location: USA (Philadelphia)

Specialties: Custom high-temp fabrics (fiberglass, aramid, carbon, silica-based) with temperature ranges from 220 °F to 3,500 °F (~120 °C to ~1925 °C), for welding/safety pads, rolls, and covers across sectors like petrochemical, shipyards, manufacturing, and rail.

alphasourceintl.com

7. Tespe srl

Location: Italy

Specialties: Technical textiles for high temperatures up to 1,400 °C using fibers like ceramic, silica, alumina, basalt, steel, PBO, aramide. Produces fabrics, ropes, sleeves, tapes, and custom handmade items.

tespe.it

8. K. Hoffmann GmbH / RBS Kompensator GmbH

Location: Germany / Austria

Specialties: High-temp fabrics (meta-/para-aramid, glass, ceramic) up to ~1,260 °C. Offers various coatings (silicone, PTFE, vermiculite, graphite). RBS focuses on expansion joints and textile insulation.

9. Kager-Hightemp (Kager Industrieprodukte GmbH)

Location: Germany

Specialties: E-glass and silicate fiber mats and tapes, with coatings like vermiculite (up to 1,300 °C), Alufix, silicone, and high-temp graphitized finishes. Applications include sealing, insulation, and thermal protection.

kager-hightemp.com

Location: Turkey (Istanbul)
